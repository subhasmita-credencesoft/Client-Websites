Nearby Places

Discover attractions around you

8 km away

ADLABS Imagica

Adlabs Imagica is a theme park near the city of Khopoli on Mumbai-Pune expressway. It features a water park, theme park, and snow park.

1 km away

Lonavala and Khandala Hill Station

Lonavala and the adjacent Khandala are beautiful, charming twin hill stations, 622 m. above sea level. They are situated in the Sahyadri ranges, located around 64 km from Pune and 96 km from Mumbai. Popular for their scenic valleys, waterfalls, and lush greenery.

64 km away

Shri Gagangiri Maharaj Ashram

Maharaj took samadhi on 4 February 2008 at his Khopoli Ashram. The Ashram provides a peaceful atmosphere for meditation and is situated amidst nature, attracting many devotees throughout the year.

6 km away

Shri Vireshwar Temple

Shri Vireshwar Temple is an ancient and revered Lord Shiva temple located amidst serene natural surroundings in Khopoli. Known for its spiritual significance and calm ambiance, the temple attracts devotees as well as visitors seeking a quiet, meditative experience close to nature.

5 km away

Zenith Waterfall

Zenith Waterfall is a popular natural attraction near Khopoli, known for its cascading waters and lush green surroundings. Best visited during the monsoon season, the waterfall offers a refreshing escape with scenic views, gentle trekking trails, and a peaceful atmosphere—perfect for nature lovers and photography enthusiasts.

10 km away

Kapari Waterfall

Kapari Waterfall is a hidden gem nestled in the Western Ghats, offering breathtaking views and a tranquil environment. Surrounded by dense greenery and rocky terrain, it is an ideal spot for adventure seekers, monsoon explorers, and guests looking to enjoy untouched natural beauty away from crowds.
