The Grand Banquet Hall

Elegance meets scale in this sophisticated, climate-controlled indoor setting. Perfectly suited for everything from royal wedding receptions to high-profile gala dinners, the hall provides a versatile canvas for your most ambitious themes and grand celebrations for up to 300 guests.

The Executive Conference Room

Equipped with high-speed Wi-Fi, an HD projector, and ergonomic layouts, it provides the perfect environment for strategy sessions and workshops for 20–40 delegates, complete with all the professional stationery and flipcharts needed to drive results.

The Poolside Deck

Whether you are hosting sundowner cocktails, themed birthday parties, or relaxed networking sessions with a view, this space accommodates up to 100 guests and features an outdoor bar setup, ambient night lighting, and direct pool access to ensure a memorable open-air experience.

The Water Park & Lawns

Perfectly designed for corporate “Family Days”, team-building retreats, or large-scale picnics, this massive outdoor space accommodates over 500 guests and features thrilling water slides and an open-air stage, providing a spectacular setting for play and celebration on a grand scale.
