Shiv Sewa is a community-driven taxi booking platform built to bring professionalism, trust, and transparency to local taxi services.

We believe that passengers deserve more than just a ride. They deserve a safe, reliable, and comfortable travel experience.

Shiv Sewa connects customers with verified local taxi drivers who are committed to delivering clean, hygienic, and well-maintained vehicles, along with courteous and professional service.

Our platform offers convenient cab services across multiple travel needs:

  • Local Pick-Up & Drop
  • Daily Rental
  • Outstation Cab Services

Unlike ride-hailing platforms that compete aggressively on price, Shiv Sewa focuses on quality, reliability, and trust. Our pricing is transparent and fair, ensuring that both customers and drivers benefit from a sustainable service model.

Safety and security remain our top priorities. Each ride is fulfilled by local drivers who know the city, respect customer expectations, and take pride in delivering dependable service.

By supporting Shiv Sewa, you are also supporting local taxi drivers who are evolving with technology to provide better service to their customers.
